What the platform is for

Mental health support, one call or click away

The National Digital Mental Health Platform is run by the Mental Health Authority to make mental health support reachable for everyone in Ghana. Through the helpline and e-Counselling, people can get confidential support, information and referral to care without having to travel or wait.

  • Listening and emotional support
  • Counselling by phone and online
  • Information about mental health
  • Referral to services near you

How it works

What happens when you reach out

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call 0800 678 678 or start an e-Counselling session.

  2. 2

    Talk it through

    Share what’s on your mind, at your own pace. You won’t be judged.

  3. 3

    Agree next steps

    Together you’ll work out what could help, from coping ideas to further support.

  4. 4

    Get connected

    If you need more care, we can refer you to services close to you.

Questions

Good to know

Your privacy matters. What you share with the helpline is treated in confidence.

Who will I talk to?

You’ll talk to a member of the Mental Health Authority’s helpline team, who is there to listen and help you find the right support.

When can I call?

The helpline is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays.

Can I call about someone else?

Yes. If you are worried about a family member, friend or colleague, call us and we’ll help you work out how to support them.

What if someone is in immediate danger?

Call 0800 678 678 straight away or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.
