What the platform is for

From your first call to a specialist who can help

The National Digital Mental Health Platform delivers national e-Counselling for Ghana. When you call the helpline or send a WhatsApp message, our team listens, assesses what you need and gives immediate support. Where more help is needed, they refer you to a mental health professional, who then contacts you directly.

  • Assessment of your needs
  • Psychological first aid in a crisis
  • Referral to psychologists, psychiatrists and other specialists
  • A specialist who reaches out to you

How e-Counselling works

What happens when you reach out

  1. 1

    Reach out

    Call 0800 678 678 or send a message on WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Assessment

    A member of the helpline team listens and works out what kind of help you need.

  3. 3

    Immediate support

    If you are in crisis, you get psychological first aid straight away.

  4. 4

    Referral

    Your details are passed securely to a psychologist, psychiatrist or other specialist.

  5. 5

    A specialist contacts you

    The specialist reaches out to you to continue your care.

The e-Counselling portal is a secure system used only by our team and specialists. You don’t need to sign up for anything; just call or message us.

Questions

Good to know

Your privacy matters. What you share with the helpline is treated in confidence.

Who will I talk to?

First, a member of the helpline team, who listens, assesses what you need and gives immediate support. If you need more help, a psychologist, psychiatrist or other specialist will then contact you.

When can I call or message?

The phone helpline and WhatsApp are both open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays.

How do I book an e-Counselling session?

You don’t need to book or sign up online. Call 0800 678 678 or message us on WhatsApp. If e-Counselling is right for you, a specialist will contact you.

Can I call about someone else?

Yes. If you are worried about a family member, friend or colleague, call us and we’ll help you work out how to support them.

What if someone is in immediate danger?

Call 0800 678 678 straight away or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.
