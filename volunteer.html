Roles & expertise

Skills we are looking for

You don’t need a health background to volunteer. Tell us what you’re good at and we’ll match you to the right activity.

Mental health professionals

Psychologists, counsellors, psychiatric nurses and doctors for screening, talks and support.

Students

Psychology, nursing, medicine, social work and other students.

Events & logistics

Setting up, ushering, registration and keeping events running smoothly.

Media & communications

Photography, video, graphic design, writing and social media.

Languages & outreach

Speakers of Ghanaian languages to reach every community.

Lived-experience advocates

People with lived experience who want to share their story and help others.

How it works

From sign-up to your first activity

  1. 1

    Register

    Fill in the short form with your details, interests and skills.

  2. 2

    Join the pool

    We review your details and add you to the volunteer pool.

  3. 3

    Get matched

    When an activity needs your skills, we contact you with the details.

  4. 4

    Orientation

    A short briefing before the activity, including how to support people safely.

  5. 5

    Volunteer & be recognised

    Take part, share feedback and receive recognition for your time.

Register

Join the volunteer pool

It takes about 5 minutes. We’ll ask for:

  • Your name, phone, email and region
  • The activities and roles you’re interested in
  • Your expertise, profession or field of study
  • Languages you speak and when you’re available

We use your details only to organise volunteering, and handle them under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). You can ask to be removed from the pool at any time.

Questions

Good to know

Have a question? Email info@mha.gov.gh.

Do I need a health or psychology background?

No. Many roles, such as events, media and outreach, need no health training. Professionals and students in related fields are also very welcome.

How much time do I need to give?

As much as you can. Most activities last a day or a few hours, and you choose which ones to join.

Will I be working with people who are unwell?

Some activities involve meeting members of the public. You’ll get a briefing first, and you’ll always work alongside MHA staff.

Can I leave the volunteer pool?

Yes, at any time. Email us and we’ll remove your details.
