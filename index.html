Who we are

A national authority for mental health

Established under the Mental Health Act, 2012 (Act 846), the Mental Health Authority is an agency of the Ministry of Health. We promote mental health and provide humane care that respects the rights of every person.

  • Set policy and standards for mental health care
  • Bring services into communities and primary care
  • Protect the rights of people receiving care
  • Lead public education against stigma
